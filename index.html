FAQ

You have questions.
We have answers.

Does Digital Dior only offer subscription packages? +
No. We offer a wide range of services that can be implemented individually as needed. We created subscription packages to provide bundled rollout solutions at a discounted rate.
What is your experience in the music industry? +
Our team brings over 50 years of combined experience. Throughout our journey, we have worked with many of the industry's leading record labels. Our collective achievements include multiple Grammy nominations, over 300 Billboard chart placements with multiple #1 records, 36 Platinum certifications, 9 Gold certifications, and 1 Diamond-certified record.
Are your marketing methods organic? +
Absolutely. We do not engage with fake metrics—this can turn detrimental for growth, performance, and algorithms. Every growth strategy is organic and designed for real, long-term fan retention.
Do you take a percentage of my masters? +
No. You retain full ownership of your masters. We only take a 10% commission on the revenue they generate—we do not own your masters in any way. If you ever leave, your masters leave with you.
How long does it take to get on Spotify after submitting a release? +
After submitting through the Artist Portal, we typically deliver your music to DSPs within 2–5 business days. Most releases go live within 3–7 days after we receive them. For best results, we recommend submitting at least 3 weeks before your target release date.
How soon can a campaign begin after signing up? +
Typically, we will have your campaign set up and running within 3–5 days depending on the service. Playlisting campaigns may take up to 7 days to start. If you have a specific deadline, let us know and we will accommodate.
What genres of music do you work with? +
We work with all genres—every form of music is welcome at Digital Dior.
